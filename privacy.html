Privacy Policy

Nexa Kingdom: Tower Defence · Last updated 21 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the game Nexa Kingdom ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by Nexaji LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom, which is the data controller. Contact: contact@nexaji.com.

1. Data we collect

DataWhenWhy
Player ID (a random account identifier)Always - created automatically on first launch, no sign-up neededTo save your kingdom on our servers and keep it in sync
Game progress: campaign results, Heroes, Pets, equipment, currencies (Valor and Ingots), cosmetics, box openings and their history, event progress, settings of your loadoutAs you playTo provide the game, keep your progress safe and prevent cheating

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it has no chat or other way to share content with other players.

2. Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded videos supplied by Google AdMob. When videos are available, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, information about the ads shown and your interactions with them, and diagnostics. Google uses this to show, measure and protect ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland we ask for your consent (through Google's consent message) before ads that use your personal data are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad the game does not ask for permission to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to Google. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

3. Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

  • Contract - to provide the game, your account, your progress and the items you obtain or buy.
  • Legitimate interests - to keep the game secure and fair, prevent fraud and cheating, and fix faults.
  • Consent - for personalised advertising where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation - to keep purchase records required for tax and accounting.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use these service providers, who process data for us or as independent controllers for their own services:

  • Supabase - hosting of our game servers, database and authentication.
  • Apple - App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple.
  • Google - Google Play purchases (Google Play Billing) and rewarded video ads (AdMob).

Some of these providers process data outside the UK and EEA, for example in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

5. How long we keep data

  • Your account and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records: kept after account deletion for up to 7 years for financial and tax records, but no longer linked to you (the player ID is removed).
  • A record that an account was deleted (containing only a one-way hash of the player ID) to prove the deletion happened.
  • Server logs: a short period set by our hosting provider.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and object to or restrict some processing. You can delete your account at any time in the game (Settings > Account > Delete Account) or by following the steps on this page. For any other request, email contact@nexaji.com. If you are unhappy with how we use your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All communication between the game and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in sessions are stored in secure device storage, and each account can only read and change its own data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the game when the change is significant.
