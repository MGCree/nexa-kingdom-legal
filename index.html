Common questions

How do I keep my progress if I change or reset my phone?

Your kingdom starts as a guest account on this device. Open Settings > Account and secure it with an email address and password (or Sign in with Apple on iPhone and iPad). On a new device, open Settings > Account > Restore Kingdom and sign in with the same details.

I forgot my password

Choose Settings > Account > Restore Kingdom > Sign in with Email > Forgot Password?. We email you a 6-digit code to set a new password.

A purchase did not arrive

Purchases are confirmed with Apple or Google before anything is added to your kingdom, which can take a moment. Keep the game open and connected. Restarting the game re-checks unfinished purchases, and Restore Purchases in the Store recovers permanent items. If it is still missing, email us with the order number from your Apple or Google receipt.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play refunds.

Box odds

Every box shows its exact odds in the game before you open or buy it.
