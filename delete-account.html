Delete your Nexa Kingdom account

Nexa Kingdom: Tower Defence, by Nexaji LTD

Delete in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Nexa Kingdom.
  2. Go to Settings > Account.
  3. Choose Delete Account and hold the button to confirm.

The account is deleted from our servers straight away and the game starts a new, empty guest account on that device. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the game's access to your Apple ID.

Request deletion without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email contact@nexaji.com with the subject "Delete my Nexa Kingdom account", from the email address you used to secure your kingdom (or, for Sign in with Apple, the email address of that Apple ID). We confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days.

A guest account that was never secured with an email or Apple sign-in cannot be identified by us; it is removed when you delete it in the game.

What is deleted and what is kept

DeletedKept
Your account and sign-in details (email, password hash, Apple link), your progress, currencies, Heroes, Pets, equipment, cosmetics, boxes and box history, event progress and rewarded-video records. Records of real-money purchases (transaction ID, product, date, status) for up to 7 years for tax and accounting, with your player ID removed so they no longer identify you; and a deletion record containing only a one-way hash of the player ID.

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases. Refunds are requested from Apple or Google.
